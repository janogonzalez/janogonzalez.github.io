Came back to Berlin for some final things. The moving truck came on Monday. Also our visas are finally ready so I can start working this month.

This week I've been trying to visit some of my favorite places before leaving and met with some friends.

My family and I were regulars at [Smart Deli][]. The owner gave me a package for them as a parting gift.

Went to [Ari's][] for the first time. This restaurant is owned by a former SoundCloud coworker and the food is great. If you like Peruvian sandwiches, this place is a must.

## Listened to

Albums/EPs:
- Favorite of the week: [Phuyu y La Fantasma - Anticuecas subterráneas (Reconstruidas)][] (Also on [Bandcamp][phuyu-bandcamp]). Re-Recording of the [original][] album with a full band this time. This modern classic of Chilean music is one of my favorite albums of 2026
- [Kaátaìra - Caminhos de água][] (Also on [Bandcamp][kaataira-bandcamp]). Brazilian masterpiece. I don't know if this is Post-Metal, Folk Metal or what, just that it's great.
- [Portrayal of Guilt - ...Beginning of the End][] (Also on [Bandcamp][portrayal-bandcamp]). I hear a lot of Godflesh influences and also Nu Metal elements

Recently released singles:
- Favorite of the week: [Deafheaven - Hunter][]. Loving this song. Leaning on the Black Metal side of the Blackgaze spectrum
- [AQROSS - Turbulencia][]. Another banger from the Chilean boy group formerly known as Q_ARE
- [Crypta - A Portrait of Decay][]. Close second favorite of the week
- [femtanyl - All the Time][]
- [IS:SUE - New Look][]
- [Itzel - TMM][]
- [Wolves in the Throne Room - Estuary][]. New album coming!

Other:
- [ARTMS - Blue Blood (DEMO ver.)][]. Due to popular demand they officially released the demo with the original drop
- [CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso - Wannabe (Spice Girls cover) in the Live Lounge][]. Mashed with Focus - Hocus Pocus!
