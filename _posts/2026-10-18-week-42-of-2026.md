Last day of freedom! Starting a new job tomorrow.

Went to the [Berlin Ramen Festival][] last Sunday. Ate ramen (duh!), takoyaki and dorayaki, also drank some sake. At some point I got into the karaoke room and stayed there until the festival was over.

Went on Monday to the [Synthesizer Museum Berlin][] and played legendary synths like the [Yamaha CS-80][] and the [Roland Jupiter-8][].

Back in Málaga, signed a lease for an apartment. Mattresses arrived on Thursday so we can sleep in the new place. Our stuff should arrive next month from Berlin.
