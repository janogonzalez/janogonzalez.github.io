## Listened to

Albums/EPs:
- Favorite of the week: [Hakushi Hasegawa - Air Ni Ni][] (Also on [Bandcamp][hakushi-bandcamp]). A masterpiece from 2019. A mix of Jazz, Pop and Electronic Music
- [idntt - \<itsnotover>][]. Another good release from this boy group
- [Inundaremos - tanquemante] (Also on [Bandcamp][inundaremos-bandcamp]). One of the best Chilean albums of 2026
- [Tokyo Shoegazer - Remains] (Also on [Bandcamp][tokyo-bandcamp]). Shoegaze from Tokyo, obviously

Recently released singles:
- Favorite of the week: [Knats - Bowling Ball][]. Jazz that hits hard
- [After - Take Me to Sunrise][]. 2000s sound
- [Balming Tiger - Janjan][]. Nice percussion sounds
- [Chat Pile - PEN I S MALL][]. The perfect song name doesn't exis...
- [CHOI YOOJUNG - Perfect Target][]. Old-school
- [Concrete Vehicles - Cut Ties][]. Runner-up for favorite single of the week
- [HANABIE - Life Is Short, O Brave Girl][]. New album coming soon!
- [idntt - Kids Return][]. A mix of classic and modern boy group sound. From the EP in the previous section
- [Mastodon - Snakes For Dinner][]. New album coming soon!
- [RESCENE - Pretty Girl][]. Cover from the 2008 [KARA][] song
- [SUNMI - Forever July][]. UKG and K-Pop is always a good mix
- [YAO - 777 (feat. Awich, CHICO CARLITO, ONE OK ROCK & Paledusk)][]
- [Yves - It][]. As usual with this artist, breaking my own rule of not mentioning new singles from a release [I already talked about][]

Other:

- [Dirty Three - Full Performance (Live on KEXP)][]. Finally got it it after some time in my watchlist
- [Napalm Death: Tiny Desk Concert][]. Most unexpected (but welcomed) Tiny Desk Concert ever?
- [Persona 3 20th Anniversary Remix][]. New arrangements for one the best gaming OSTs
- [T.M.Revolution - HOT LIMIT / THE FIRST TAKE][]. A classic Japanese summer song
