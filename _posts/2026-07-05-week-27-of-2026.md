Signed an offer this week. So glad that the search is finally over. Will post more details soonish.

Spent a lot of hours this week building a Steam Machine++ with a [RX 9070 XT][] inside a [Fractal Design Ridge][]. The build runs [SteamOS][] 3.8 for a console-like experience when gaming. Still tweaking settings to improve noise and thermal performance even more, which can't be further from a console-like experience 😆. I used an LLM to help me tweak configuration, if any component ends up fried that's on me 😅.
