Finished my third week at the new job. Everything going well so far, but I don't have much time to listen to music properly now.

## Listened to

Albums/EPs:
- Favorite of the week: [Légamo - Círculo de fuego][]. First time listening to this Chilean Jazz Fusion band. Another great Chilean release. My only complaint is the length, only 30 minutes
- [Corretta - A New Age of Decay][] (Also on [Bandcamp][corretta-bandcamp]). A Spotify recommendation. Nice Prog Metal
- [Estoy Bien - A La Distancia][]. Good stuff but their [previous album][] is even better (maybe the best Chilean Emo album?)
- [RESCENE - Pulse][]. Solid EP with a nice RnB sound. They played it a little bit too safe, hopefully they'll take more risks in the next release

Recently released singles:
- Favorite of the week: [Panchiko - One Car Hits Another (feat. Mei Semones)][]. Unexpected collab
- [Baby DONT Cry - Replay][]. The song is indeed replayable
- [cosmosy - Give Me Love][]. This goes hard
- [Genevieve Artadi - Ode to Spaghetti][]. An ode to a dog
- [HEART OF WOMAN - Melt][]. DnB vibes
- [My New Band Believe - Opposite Teacher][]. Breaking once again my rule of not mentioning singles from albums already mentioned. They released a music video produced in Chile!
- [Raised by Owls - My Optician Called Me A Poser Because I Couldn't Read The Sanguisugabogg Logo][]. For this one, you need to watch the music video for the full experience
- [Slayyyter - hey boyyy][]. She continues slaying
- [SPOKANKI - Tis Trihas to Gefyri][]. I don't know how this type of music is called but it's pretty cool
- [Yaeji - ddr][]. Dance Dance Revolution, not Deutsche Demokratische Republik


Other:

- [Asia Menor - Vi al diablo + Flores del naranjo + La Naturaleza (En Rojas Magallanes)][]
- ["Be Quiet and Drive (Far Away)" Under a Bridge][]. This random cover made me feel really nostalgic. Maybe it's the video quality or the crowd energy. It looks like if it was recorded at the time the song was released
- [NewJeans - OMG \| RE:GROOVE Live Session]. Nice cover
